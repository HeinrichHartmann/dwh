## Format

Each ADR follows this structure:

```markdown

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
Why is this decision needed?

## Decision
What is the decision?

## Consequences
What are the results?
```
